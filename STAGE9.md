# GOAT Cricket 3D — Stage 9 / Final Gameplay Core

Stage 9 is the final planned prototype milestone. It connects the batting contact layer to a deterministic cricket delivery loop and a persistent innings state.

## Included
- Pace/swing/seam/spin/yorker/bouncer/slower/cutter delivery types.
- Ball outcomes: dot, 1/2/3, four, six, wide, no-ball, wicket, bye and leg-bye engine types.
- Legal-ball and over tracking.
- Free-hit state after no-ball.
- Striker/non-striker rotation.
- Target and required-rate calculation.
- Batting timing, power and direction passed into contact resolution.
- Wicket/edge logic tied to bowling difficulty.
- Dynamic commentary based on actual outcome and chase pressure.
- Existing Stage 1–8 systems retained.

## Reality check
This remains a browser-game prototype. Production-quality photorealistic character rigs, motion-capture animation, networking and server persistence still require real art/engineering assets. Stage 9 does not claim those are automatically generated.
